Game Mechanics:

- You control a catcher box near the bottom of the screen. Clicking anywhere on the screen instantly

- You start with 3 hearts (shown top-right). The game ends when all 3 hearts are lost.

Your objective: Maximize your score by catching as many ingredients as possible while avoiding bombs
